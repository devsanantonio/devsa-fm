Title:
DEVSA FM · Christmas in San Antonio, in ASCII · Tejano lofi for learners and builders

Description:
DEVSA FM's Christmas edition: Tejano lofi radio for San Antonio's learners and builders, live through the holidays.

Press play and keep building. On screen, Christmas in San Antonio, drawn in ASCII with pixel snow, and every scene moves to its own song:
· the DEVSA tree, from the DEVSA TV studio desk
· the ride downtown: the bus in a holiday wrap, past Mission San José and the boots outlined in lights
· the gift station at Market Square: under papel picado and a lit tree, the agents wrap, check and ship seven gifts into Santa's sleigh, Tux keeps the log, and Santa walks in to climb aboard
· movie night at Mission Marquee Plaza: the old drive-in screen in lights, the cast on blankets and lawn chairs, and on the big screen devsa.community's "Where San Antonio Builds." over DEVSA's event photos, then a countdown and trailers for localhost:210, PySanAntonio, Access Granted, The Model and More Human Than Human, while the penguin keeps the cocoa stand
· the DEVSA Express rolling past the Alamodome, a holiday train carrying every community group in DEVSA's network, the cast waving from the crossing
· a Christmas show at the Arneson River Theatre in La Villita: the PySanAntonio mascot on guitar and Linux San Antonio's penguin on keys under the lit bell arches, the river boats carrying elves between the stage and the steps
· San Fernando Cathedral's Christmas light show: luminarias, a nochebuena, papel picado, ornaments and a DEVSA terminal
· Christmas on Houston Street: the Rand Building with its 1913 clock and a tree in the lobby, wreaths and garland, the street trees wrapped in lights, its third floor lit for a DEVSA meetup, and the cast walking in with laptops and tamales
· the Alamo Python taco truck, wrapped in lights, with conchas on the board and the penguin shipping an MCP server for the DEVSA calendar
· and a New Year finale: the DEVSA mark and pixel fireworks

The edition goes round every 20 minutes.

Build a scene for the stream. DEVSA FM is open source, and every scene is one HTML file:
