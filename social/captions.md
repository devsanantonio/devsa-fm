## LinkedIn

Put it on while you build: DEVSA FM is Tejano lofi radio for San Antonio's learners and builders, streaming on YouTube.

On screen is a night in San Antonio, drawn in ASCII, with a new scene for every song:

→ a night bus past Mission San José and the big boots
→ glowing fish under the bridge on the Museum Reach, a nod to Donald Lipski's F.I.S.H.
→ a basketball game between two AI agents, Patch and Lint. Patch runs a ReAct loop, Lint runs reflection: it critiques every late contest, learns to block, and wins at the buzzer
→ midnight at the Alamo Python food truck, where the Linux San Antonio penguin ships a localhost:210 starter, failing test and all
→ the PySanAntonio mascot playing in front of the Rand Building, its windows lighting up on the song's beat
→ the River Walk at night

Every frame is drawn in code: the browser paints each scene in characters, timed to the music.

Music: "Tejano Lofi" by Patrick, made with Suno.
