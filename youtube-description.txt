Title:
DEVSA FM · A night in San Antonio, in ASCII · Tejano lofi for learners and builders

Description:
DEVSA FM: Tejano lofi radio for San Antonio's learners and builders.

Press play and keep building. On screen, a night in San Antonio drawn in ASCII, and every scene moves to its own song:
· the night bus, rolling past Mission San José and the big boots
· glowing fish under the bridge on the Museum Reach, a nod to Donald Lipski's F.I.S.H.
· a game between two agents, Patch and Lint, down to the buzzer
· midnight at the Alamo Python taco truck, shipping a localhost:210 starter
· a concert in front of the Rand Building
· NEW: lotería at Market Square, with a conjunto under the lamp post and a deck of DEVSA's own cards
· NEW: San Fernando Cathedral, lit by a projection show mapped to its stone
· the River Walk at night, with the agents now riding the barge

The night goes round about every 24 minutes.

Build a scene for the stream. DEVSA FM is open source, and every scene is one HTML file:
https://github.com/devsanantonio/devsa-fm
